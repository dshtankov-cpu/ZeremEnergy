# Zerem Energy SEO roadmap

## Implemented technical foundation

- Unique titles, descriptions, canonicals and Open Graph metadata.
- Crawlable `robots.txt` and XML sitemap.
- Organization and WebSite structured data on the homepage.
- Service and BreadcrumbList structured data on service pages.
- Branded 404 page and permanent `/home` redirect.
- Internal links to crawlable service pages in the homepage and footer.
- Descriptive image alternative text where semantic images are used.
- English service pages for generator rental and temporary power.

## Next content priorities

1. Confirm the legal company name, real address, phone, email and social profiles.
2. Add real project case studies with capacity, region, challenge, system design and outcome.
3. Add original equipment and installation photography with accurate captions.
4. Create English pages for gas and hybrid power, EPC and O&M, then review for distinct search intent.
5. After English approval, publish a complete Hebrew version with RTL layout and reciprocal `hreflang` links.
6. Create a verified Google Business Profile when the real business address and contacts are ready.
7. Connect Google Search Console, submit `/sitemap.xml`, request indexing and monitor queries, pages, countries and AI-feature visibility.
8. Add a privacy policy before enabling the enquiry form; include the policy link and consent text in the form.

## Content rules

- Publish only facts Zerem Energy can verify.
- Avoid mass-producing near-duplicate city pages.
- Give each page one distinct search intent and link it from relevant pages.
- Prefer engineering detail, real examples, specifications and project evidence over repeated keyword phrases.
- Keep structured data consistent with visible page content.
